# Uraan — Presentation outline (5 slides) + English walkthrough script

## Slide 1 — The problem and the promise

**Title:** "Which route abroad is real for me — and what's missing?"

- Bangladeshi students start 12–24 months early, buried in YouTube advice
  and scholarship aggregators that overpromise.
- Uraan answers one question honestly: *given my education, budget,
  language ability, and intake, which routes are plausible, what's
  missing, and what should I do next?*
- Screenshot: home page (EN) — one-sentence outcome, "Build my
  preparation plan" + "Explore opportunities".

## Slide 2 — Verified facts, honest matching

**Title:** "11 real opportunities, checked against official sources"

- Coverage: US · Germany · Japan, bachelor's/master's/PhD — MEXT, Fulbright
  (Bangladesh), Erasmus Mundus, DAAD EPOS/Helmut Schmidt/Research Grants,
  MIT & Harvard aid, UTokyo PEAK, Knight-Hennessy.
- Every fact carries its official source, retrieval date, and review
  state. Closed cycles render closed. Unknown stays unknown.
- Screenshot: opportunity detail with the deterministic match — "12 years
  met · embassy screening needs confirmation" and the conservative note.

## Slide 3 — A plan you can actually follow

**Title:** "A dated, capacity-aware preparation plan"

- Deterministic engine back-plans curated templates from the target
  intake, adds gap-driven tasks, and refuses to silently compress an
  infeasible schedule.
- Guests explore without sign-in; one click merges everything into an
  account exactly once. Progress survives reloads and devices.
- Screenshot: My Journey dashboard — "1 of 20 tasks done", top-3 next
  actions, requirement gaps, upcoming dates.

## Slide 4 — Preparation tools with real AI

**Title:** "Workspaces that build the real you — with AI that knows its place"

- Language study log + practice feedback (clearly labelled: not an
  IELTS score). Writing editor with autosave, versions, and AI feedback
  that flags unsupported claims — disabled when a target prohibits AI.
- Experience journal counts genuine work, family duties, and projects.
  Private document storage with owner-only access and real deletion.
- Screenshot: writing workspace with live Bangla AI feedback card.

## Slide 5 — Honest by design

**Title:** "What we will never do"

- No admission/scholarship/visa promises, no invented deadlines, no fake
  testimonials, no "best country" rankings.
- Competitive awards never counted as guaranteed funds; unknown costs keep
  totals honest. Editorial review before any published change; users
  notified, plans never silently rewritten.
- Full EN/BN interface. Open source, honest release report in the repo.
- Screenshot: Sources & coverage page (Bangla) + QR/link to the live app.

## English walkthrough script (~3 minutes)

> This is **Uraan** — a study-abroad preparation workspace built for
> Bangladeshi students.
>
> I arrive with no account. The home page says exactly what I get: my real
> route, my gaps, my next step. I click **Build my preparation plan**.
>
> Onboarding is conversational — degree level, interests, target intake,
> citizenship, my HSC with grades exactly as printed — Uraan never
> converts my scale — languages, honest family budget, and how many hours
> a week I can actually give. Everything autosaves; I can skip what I
> don't know yet.
>
> Now **Explore**: eleven real, verified opportunities across the US,
> Germany, and Japan — MEXT, Fulbright for Bangladesh, Erasmus Mundus,
> DAAD programmes, MIT and Harvard aid. Each one shows its official
> sources, when the facts were checked, and what's still unknown. A
> closed cycle says closed.
>
> I open **MEXT Undergraduate** and click **Check my fit**. This is a
> deterministic engine, not vibes: my twelve years of schooling — met.
> Embassy screening — needs confirmation with the embassy, because that's
> the truth. Missing pieces show as missing, never as passed. And the app
> reminds me: meeting every documented check does not guarantee anything.
>
> I set it as my target and generate my plan — twenty dated tasks back-
> planned from the intake, with the first gaps to resolve this month. The
> engine checks my weekly capacity and tells me honestly if the schedule
> doesn't fit. I complete my first task — one of twenty — and it stays
> done.
>
> I create an account. One explicit question: move my guest work into the
> account? One click — everything transfers exactly once, nothing
> duplicated.
>
> Under **Prepare** this is where it gets real. I log language study time.
> I write a practice paragraph and get **live AI feedback** — labelled as
> practice, never as an official score. In the writing workspace, my
> essay autosaves with version history, and AI feedback flags claims I
> can't support — grounded in my own profile facts. If a target programme
> prohibits AI assistance, the feedback button switches itself off. I stay
> the author. My experience journal records genuine work and family
> responsibilities, not certificate-collecting. Documents I upload are
> private to me alone, and deletion really deletes.
>
> I track my real applications through their actual states, export my
> calendar — date-only, no invented time zones — and my full plan.
>
> And the entire interface is **fully available in Bangla** — real
> translations, not placeholders.
>
> Behind the scenes, an editorial pipeline: allowlisted official sources,
> fetch health, human review before any fact changes, audit trail, and
> in-app notifications for affected plans. AI explains deterministic
> results; it never overrides them.
>
> Uraan never promises admission, scholarships, or visas. It promises the
> truth about where you stand, and a realistic path forward. That's what a
> student actually needs. Thank you.
